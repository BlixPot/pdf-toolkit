PDF Toolkit: compress PDF to an exact size, offline

Compress PDF to 100 KB, 200 KB or 1 MB. Merge, split, edit and sign PDFs. Free Android app that works without internet and never uploads your files.

Get it on Google Play

  • Exact size: type the KB or MB you need and get a file at or just under it.
  • Private: no upload, no account. Documents stay on your phone.
  • 13 tools: compress, increase size, merge, split, edit, organize pages, image to PDF, PDF to image, lock, unlock, watermark, page numbers, PDF to text.

How it works

  1. Install PDF Toolkit from Google Play and open it.
  2. Choose Compress PDF, pick your PDF, and type the size you need.
  3. Tap Compress. The file is saved on your phone, ready to upload or share.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a PDF to 100 KB or 200 KB?

Open Compress PDF, pick your file, choose Exact size and type the size. The app produces a file at or just under it.

Is it really offline?

Yes. PDF, image and text processing runs on your phone. The app connects to the internet only to show ads.

Is PDF Toolkit free?

Yes. It is free and supported by ads.

Do I need to sign up?

No account, no registration.
