Edit a PDF on your phone: write, draw, highlight, sign

Fill in a form, add a signature by drawing it, or highlight important lines. Tap to place text and drag it where it belongs.

Get it on Google Play

Your original file is never changed; the edited copy is saved separately.

Text and drawings are added as an overlay on the page, so the file looks the same on every device.

How it works

  1. Open Edit PDF and choose a file.
  2. Pick Pen, Highlight, Text or Eraser and mark the pages.
  3. Tap Save PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the existing text in the PDF?

Not yet. You can add new text, drawings and highlights on top of the page.

Is PDF Toolkit free?

Yes. It is free and supported by ads.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs on your phone. The app only goes online to show ads.

Do I need to sign up?

No account, no registration.
