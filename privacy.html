﻿# Privacy Policy – PDF Toolkit

Last updated: 30 October 2026

PDF Toolkit ("the app") is a tool for compressing, merging, splitting and editing PDF files on your device.

Your files stay on your device

All PDF, image and text processing happens **locally on your phone**. The app has no server and no user

accounts. We do not upload, store, view or share your documents.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The app shows ads using **Google AdMob**, which may

collect and use the following data according to Google's policies:

  • Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP
  • Device and app information, usage and diagnostics data

You can learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad

personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads) or inside the app under

Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown where required by law).

For users in the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland the app asks for consent through

Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state – only to load advertisements.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save the files you create into the Downloads folder.

On newer Android versions no storage permission is needed.

  • Advertising ID – used by the ad provider for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Data retention and deletion

Files created by the app are stored in the app's private storage and in the folders you choose to save to.

You can delete them any time from the My Files tab or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data

about you, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
