Compress PDF to 50 KB on your phone

Type 50 KB, pick your PDF and get a file at or just under that size. Works offline, with no upload and no sign-up.

Get it on Google Play

When you need 50 KB

50 KB is a common ceiling for single-page attachments. Text stays readable on a clean scan, but photos will look soft.

Typical uses: small form attachments such as an ID page, a mark sheet or a one-page certificate. Always check the exact limit and file rules on the portal you are using.

How it works

  1. Install PDF Toolkit from Google Play and open it.
  2. Choose Compress PDF, pick your PDF, and type the size you need.
  3. Tap Compress. The file is saved on your phone, ready to upload or share.

Tips for a clear 50 KB PDF

  • Aim at one to three pages. A 10-page scan at 50 KB will be hard to read.
  • Use Exact size mode and enter 50 KB; the app stops just under it so you keep as much quality as possible.
  • Check the preview before you upload: open the result in the built-in reader and zoom in on the text.
Good to know: to reach a small size, PDF Toolkit stores each page as an optimised image, so text in the compressed copy is no longer selectable. Your original file is never changed.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my 50 KB PDF blurry?

Every page is stored as an image to reach the size. Fewer pages, or a sharper original, gives a clearer result.

Does the file ever go above 50 KB?

No. The result is at or just under 50 KB. If a file cannot get that small at a readable quality, the app tells you and shows the smallest it could make.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs on your phone. The app only goes online to show ads.

Do I need to sign up?

No account, no registration.
