Merge PDF files into one, offline

Join certificates, bills or scanned pages into a single PDF in the order you choose. Add as many files as you like, move them up or down, and merge.

Get it on Google Play

Because the work is done on your phone, nothing is uploaded to a website. You can combine private documents safely.

Merge first, then compress, if the final file has to meet a size limit.

How it works

  1. Open Merge PDF and tap Add PDFs.
  2. Use the arrows to set the order.
  3. Tap Merge PDFs, then save or share the result.
