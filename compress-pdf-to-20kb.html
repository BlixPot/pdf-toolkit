Compress PDF to 20 KB on your phone

Type 20 KB, pick your PDF and get a file at or just under that size. Works offline, with no upload and no sign-up.

Get it on Google Play

When you need 20 KB

A 20 KB limit is the tightest you will usually meet. It is realistic for one or two simple pages, not for a long scanned document.

Typical uses: very small uploads such as a signature page or a single-page certificate. Always check the exact limit and file rules on the portal you are using.

How it works

  1. Install PDF Toolkit from Google Play and open it.
  2. Choose Compress PDF, pick your PDF, and type the size you need.
  3. Tap Compress. The file is saved on your phone, ready to upload or share.

Tips for a clear 20 KB PDF

  • Compress only the pages you must submit; fewer pages means each page gets a bigger share of the 20 KB.
  • Black-and-white or text-only pages shrink far better than colour photos.
  • If the result is too blurry, scan or export the source again at a lower resolution instead of squeezing a huge file.
Good to know: to reach a small size, PDF Toolkit stores each page as an optimised image, so text in the compressed copy is no longer selectable. Your original file is never changed.

Frequently asked questions

Can a multi-page scan fit in 20 KB?

Usually not at a readable quality. Split out the pages you need first (Split PDF) and compress those.

Does the file ever go above 20 KB?

No. The result is at or just under 20 KB. If a file cannot get that small at a readable quality, the app tells you and shows the smallest it could make.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs on your phone. The app only goes online to show ads.

Do I need to sign up?

No account, no registration.
