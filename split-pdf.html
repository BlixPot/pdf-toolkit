Split a PDF by page ranges, offline

Pull out only the pages you need. Type ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8-10, split every N pages, or turn every page into its own PDF.

Get it on Google Play

Choose whether each range becomes its own file or all ranges go into one new PDF.

Splitting before you compress gives each page a bigger share of a tight size limit.

How it works

  1. Open Split PDF and pick your file.
  2. Type the pages you want, for example 1-3, 5.
  3. Tap Split PDF and save the new files.

Frequently asked questions

Is the original file changed?
