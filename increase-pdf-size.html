Increase PDF size to an exact KB or MB

Some portals reject files that are too small as well as too large. PDF Toolkit can grow a PDF to a minimum size such as 100 KB, 300 KB or 2 MB without changing a single page.

Get it on Google Play

It adds invisible padding data to the file, so text, images and layout stay exactly as they were. The final size matches the number you enter.

Everything happens on your phone, so private documents are never uploaded.

How it works

  1. Open Increase PDF Size in PDF Toolkit.
  2. Pick your PDF and enter the size you need, for example 200 KB.
  3. Tap Increase size and upload the result.

Frequently asked questions

Does this change my pages?

No. The pages, text and images are untouched; only extra invisible data is added.

Is PDF Toolkit free?

Yes. It is free and supported by ads.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs on your phone. The app only goes online to show ads.

Do I need to sign up?

No account, no registration.
