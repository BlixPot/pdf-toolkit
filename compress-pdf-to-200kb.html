Compress PDF to 200 KB on your phone

Type 200 KB, pick your PDF and get a file at or just under that size. Works offline, with no upload and no sign-up.

Get it on Google Play

When you need 200 KB

200 KB gives you room for a few pages at a clearly readable quality, which is why many upload portals pick it as a limit.

Typical uses: job and exam applications, address proofs and multi-page certificates. Always check the exact limit and file rules on the portal you are using.

How it works

  1. Install PDF Toolkit from Google Play and open it.
  2. Choose Compress PDF, pick your PDF, and type the size you need.
  3. Tap Compress. The file is saved on your phone, ready to upload or share.

Tips for a clear 200 KB PDF

  • Four to eight clean pages usually fit; image-heavy pages need more room.
  • Choose Exact size and type 200 KB. The result is at or just under the target, never above it.
  • Keep the original file. Compressing a compressed file again only loses quality.
Good to know: to reach a small size, PDF Toolkit stores each page as an optimised image, so text in the compressed copy is no longer selectable. Your original file is never changed.

Frequently asked questions

Is 200 KB enough for a 5-page PDF?

Often yes if the pages are mostly text. Try it: the result screen shows the final size next to the original.

Does the file ever go above 200 KB?

No. The result is at or just under 200 KB. If a file cannot get that small at a readable quality, the app tells you and shows the smallest it could make.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs on your phone. The app only goes online to show ads.

Do I need to sign up?

No account, no registration.
